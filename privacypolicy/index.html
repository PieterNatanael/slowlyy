Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2025

🎯 Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Slowlyy, we believe your privacy is fundamental. This Privacy Policy explains how our cryptocurrency wallet application ("Slowlyy Wallet") handles your information. The short version: we don't collect, store, or share your personal data.

Privacy First: Slowlyy Wallet is designed with a "privacy-by-design" approach. Your wallet data never leaves your device except for necessary blockchain interactions.

📱 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your seed phrases or private keys
  • Your wallet addresses or transaction history
  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Biometric data (Face ID/Touch ID stays on your device)
  • Any passwords or authentication credentials

🔒 How Your Data Is Stored

  • iOS Keychain: Seed phrase and pending transactions stored securely with hardware-level encryption
  • Biometric Protection: Access requires Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode
  • No Cloud Backup: Sensitive data is never backed up to iCloud or other cloud services

🌐 Network Communications

  • Polygon Blockchain via Alchemy API (no personal data)
  • Time verification via timeapi.io (no personal data)
  • No tracking or linking to your identity

⏰ Delayed Transaction Feature

  • Pending transactions stored locally in secure keychain
  • No transaction data sent to servers
  • Time verification ensures security without compromising privacy

🧪 Testnet Usage

  • All transactions use test tokens with no real-world value
  • Testnet data is public on the blockchain but not linked to your identity
  • No financial or personal risk

👨‍💻 Third-Party Services

  • Alchemy: Blockchain interactions (wallet addresses only)
  • TimeAPI.io: UTC time verification (no user data transmitted)
  • App Store: Standard iOS app distribution
  • Trust WalletCore: Industry-standard algorithms

🔐 Your Security Responsibilities

  • Keep your seed phrase secure and private
  • Do not share device passcode or biometric access
  • Understand lost seed phrases cannot be recovered
  • Use the app responsibly on testnet

🔄 Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted in the app and on this page. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

⚠️ Disclaimers

  • Slowlyy Wallet is for educational and testing purposes
  • Testnet software — tokens have no real-world value
  • Use at your own risk
  • Not responsible for lost access due to forgotten credentials
