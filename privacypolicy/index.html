Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 August 2025

1. Information We Collect

Slowlyy is a self-custodial wallet. This means:

  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your private keys, seed phrases, wallet balances, or transaction history.
  • All sensitive information is encrypted and stored locally on your device.
  • We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal identifiers (such as name, email, or phone number) unless you provide them for support purposes.

2. How We Use Information

If any information is collected (such as optional diagnostic data or support requests), we only use it to:

  • Provide and improve Slowlyy
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests
  • Enhance app performance and security

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Slowlyy may use trusted third-party services for analytics and crash reporting. These services may collect non-personal information such as device type, operating system, and app usage patterns. No sensitive wallet data is ever shared.

4. Security

Your private keys and seed phrases are only stored locally on your device. We strongly encourage you to back up your seed phrase securely offline. Slowlyy cannot recover your wallet if you lose your device or recovery phrase.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access information we hold about you (if any)
  • Request deletion of support-related data
  • Withdraw consent to optional data collection

For requests, contact us at: pieternatanael@icloud.com

6. Children’s Privacy

Slowlyy is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in the app and/or on our website with a revised “Effective Date.”
