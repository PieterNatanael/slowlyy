The Problem

Regular self-custody online wallets have a major problem: If the device is hacked, stolen, or compromised, the assets can be drained fast, even with passwords and 2FA.

Solution: Slowlyy

Slowlyy is a self-custody online wallet with a 3–30 day delay that protects your crypto from hacks, theft, and costly mistakes.

How It Works: Testnet vs Mainnet

🧪 Testnet (v1) — Live Now

  • Experience a 30 minute delay and see slow transfers in action.
  • Use testnet funds, no real value at risk.
  • Seed phrase shown only once, store it safely offline as a last resort.

🛡️ Mainnet — In Development

  • User-configurable 3–30 day delay for stronger protection.
  • Planned support for Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, and Cardano (more to come).
  • Ongoing security hardening and UX refinements.

Benefits

  • Available on mobile, laptop, and desktop for secure online access anytime.
  • No third-party custody: users own their private keys.
  • Tamper-resistant validation via dual sources and cryptographic signatures.
  • Time-based protection that gives you the power to cancel suspicious transactions.
  • Peace of mind knowing your assets can't be drained instantly.
