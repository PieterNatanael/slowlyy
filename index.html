The Problem

Crypto holders constantly worry about the safety of their assets. One wrong click or download could expose their wallets — and most people feel helpless or unsure how to protect themselves. They’ve heard that keeping crypto in a hot wallet is unsafe — but that’s like telling someone not to carry cash in their handbag and blaming them if it gets stolen. The fear of losing everything in minutes creates anxiety about keeping crypto online, even though they still want the convenience of managing it on their devices.

Solution: Slowlyy

Most people rely on regular wallets with passwords or two-factor authentication — better than nothing, but still vulnerable. Instead of forcing users to change how they store crypto, we built a smarter layer of protection. Our system locks transactions behind a configurable delay, meaning crypto can’t be moved — by anyone, including the owner — until the set time passes. This gives users real security without losing the freedom and convenience they expect.

How It Works: Testnet vs Mainnet

🧪 Testnet (v1) — Live Now

  • Experience a 30-minute delay and see slow transfers in action.
  • Use testnet funds — no real value at risk.
  • Seed phrase shown only once — store it safely offline as a last resort.
  • If something goes wrong, time itself offers protection.

🛡️ Mainnet — In Development

  • User-configurable 3–30 day delay for stronger protection.
  • Planned support for Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, and Cardano (more to come).
  • Ongoing security hardening and UX refinements.

Benefits

  • Available on mobile, laptop, and desktop for secure access anytime.
  • No third-party custody: users own their private keys.
  • Tamper-resistant validation via dual sources and cryptographic signatures.
