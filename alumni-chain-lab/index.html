Why Alumni Chain Lab Exists

Apple Developer Academy alumni are on many paths including job seekers, freelancers, founders, and full-time engineers.

Alumni Chain Lab brings together alumni who share an interest in blockchain, crypto systems, and security-focused engineering. It provides a shared space for real, visible, collaborative work where members can learn, contribute, and stay sharp at any stage of their career.

Ways to Participate

Alumni Chain Lab is designed to be flexible. Members can participate in different ways depending on their time, interests, and experience.

  • Build — Contribute code, prototypes, smart contracts, or experiments to active projects such as Slowlyy.
  • Review — Review pull requests, architecture decisions, security considerations, or documentation.
  • Mentor — Share experience, give feedback, or guide other alumni through technical or career challenges.
  • Observe — Follow discussions, explore the codebase, and learn at your own pace, no pressure to contribute immediately.

Projects Within Alumni Chain Lab

Alumni Chain Lab hosts one or more active projects where members learn by contributing to real, production-level systems.

  • Slowlyy — Founding project of Alumni Chain Lab, used as a real-world crypto wallet for learning about security, delay mechanisms, architecture, and UX.
  • Additional alumni-led projects may be added over time, proposed and reviewed by members of the lab.

Alumni Chain Lab is not limited to one product. The lab exists to support learning through multiple real projects over time.

Express Interest

Alumni Chain Lab is open to Apple Developer Academy alumni who are interested in blockchain, crypto systems, and security-focused engineering, and who want to learn by participating in real projects.

This is an alumni-led learning initiative. It is not a job offer, not an investment opportunity, and not a token or fundraising project.
