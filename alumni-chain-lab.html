Alumni Chain Lab

An independent, alumni-only builders lab where Apple Developer Academy graduates learn blockchain by collaborating on real products and shipping production-grade code.

What is Alumni Chain Lab Join (Alumni Only)

Overview

Alumni Chain Lab is an independent initiative founded by Apple Developer Academy alumni. It exists to help alumni continue growing as builders by working on real blockchain-enabled products in a trusted, professional environment.

This is not a classroom, bootcamp, or investment group. It is a working product lab focused on collaboration, code review, and shipping.

Why Alumni Chain Lab Exists

  • Support alumni affected by the tech hiring slowdown
  • Provide verifiable, portfolio-ready experience
  • Maintain momentum through real collaboration
  • Build a high-signal alumni builders network

How It Works

  • Members collaborate on real production codebases
  • Work is proposed, reviewed, and merged via GitHub
  • Security and user safety are mandatory considerations
  • Production-ready contributions may be shipped publicly

Flagship Product Lab: Slowlyy

Slowlyy is a real, alumni-founded product used as the primary learning and contribution platform inside Alumni Chain Lab.

Members gain exposure to real architecture decisions, security trade-offs, and production constraints — experience rarely found in personal projects.

Membership

  • Apple Developer Academy alumni only
  • Verification required
  • Public recognition is optional
  • Contributors receive public credit for shipped work

Alumni Chain Lab is independent and not affiliated with Apple or the Apple Developer Academy.
