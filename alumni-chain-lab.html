Alumni Chain Lab

A private collaboration space where Apple Developer Academy alumni learn by building real blockchain and crypto products together, from product thinking to shipped code.

Express Interest Learn More

Important: Alumni Chain Lab is alumni-led and independent. It is not affiliated with Apple Inc. or the Apple Developer Academy.

Inside the lab

Learn on real products, not classroom exercises.

  • Build alongside alumni across cohorts and countries.
  • Contribute to active products such as Slowlyy.
  • Develop portfolio material from real reviews and shipped work.
Why it exists

A place for alumni who still want to build together.

Alumni Chain Lab exists for Academy graduates who want a serious space to keep learning through real work, especially in blockchain, crypto systems, security, and product execution.

Apple Developer Academy alumni move into many different roles across design, engineering, product, and business. What often disappears after graduation is a shared place to keep building across disciplines.

Alumni Chain Lab is designed to keep that collaboration alive. Instead of creating exercises or simulations, the lab centers itself around active projects where members can learn by participating in real product decisions and implementation.

What the lab is not

It is not a token launch, not a fundraising vehicle, and not a formal employer. It is an alumni-led initiative built around contribution, review, and learning through real product work.

Ways to participate

Different levels of involvement, one real workflow.

Members can join according to their time, skillset, and curiosity. The lab is designed so people can contribute deeply or observe first and grow into active work over time.

  • Build by contributing code, prototypes, smart contracts, research, or experiments to active projects.
  • Review architecture, pull requests, security decisions, UX flows, and documentation.
  • Mentor by sharing experience and guiding other alumni through technical or product challenges.
  • Observe discussions, study the codebase, and learn the domain before contributing directly.
What members get

Learn, collaborate, ship.

Learn

Collaborate

Work across cohorts

Ship

Contribute publicly

Projects

Real products inside the lab.

Alumni Chain Lab is not intended to stay tied to a single project forever, but it starts with one serious product and expands from there.

  • Slowlyy is the founding project of the lab and gives contributors exposure to security-focused wallet design, delayed settlement, architecture, and UX trade-offs.
  • Additional alumni-led projects can be proposed, discussed, and added over time as the lab grows.

The goal is not to collect side projects. The goal is to learn through products that are concrete enough to deserve real engineering discipline.

Portfolio value

Work that can be spoken about seriously.

  • Reference concrete contributions in CVs, portfolios, and interviews.
  • Link to real GitHub pull requests, reviews, or product outcomes where appropriate.
  • Show experience with actual users, real constraints, and production-minded systems.

Contributors can reference approved work under roles such as iOS Engineer, UX/UI Designer, Product Manager, Backend Developer, Blockchain Engineer, or Security Researcher, depending on the work actually completed.

Express interest

Alumni Chain Lab is open to Apple Developer Academy alumni who want to explore blockchain, crypto systems, security-focused engineering, and product development through real collaborative work.

This is an alumni-led learning initiative. It is not a job offer, not an investment opportunity, and not a token or fundraising project.
